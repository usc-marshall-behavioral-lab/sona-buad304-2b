What Step 2B Is

The part students worry about most, and simpler than it looks.

You provide contact information for three employees at the same organization. You enter their names and work emails, and the research team handles everything else. Your contacts receive a short, confidential email survey about workplace dynamics. Most people are happy to help when asked.

Your role ends at submission. Once you enter the contacts in SONA, Step 2B is complete and you can begin receiving Step 3 study invitations. The research team sends the surveys, collects responses, and follows up.

Everything is confidential. Individual survey responses are never shared with employers, coworkers, or you.

How long it actually takes

SONA lists about 10 minutes, and the form itself is that fast. The real timeline includes finding the right person and waiting for a reply.

Don't wait until the last day. The earlier you ask, the more time your contacts have to respond.

Who You Need

Three people from the same organization, each working 30+ hours a week.

1

Focal employee

Your main contact, someone you know personally. You reach out to them, and they help coordinate the other two.

2

Their coworker

Someone at the same level as your focal employee, at the same organization.

3

Their supervisor

Someone who manages or oversees the focal employee's work. A direct or indirect manager both count.

Key rule: same organization. All three must work at the same company or organization, and each needs a valid work email address, not a personal Gmail or Yahoo account.

What you need for each person

  • Full name
  • Work email address
  • Job title
  • Their role: focal employee, coworker, or supervisor

Who does not qualify

  • Part-time employees. All three work 30+ hours a week.
  • Personal email addresses. Work emails only.
  • Different organizations. All three from the same workplace.
  • Yourself or other students.
  • Contacts from a previous semester. Submissions must be new.

Where to Find Contacts

Think of anyone you know who works at an organization with at least three employees.

  • Current or past employer
  • Internship
  • A family member's workplace
  • A friend's workplace
  • Campus job, at USC or elsewhere
  • Volunteer organization

You only contact one person. Ask your focal employee for a coworker's and a supervisor's name and work email. Most people can handle it in a single text.

Common Scenarios

Not sure your situation qualifies? You are probably not the first to ask.

I only have internship experience.

Internships count. As long as your contacts work 30+ hours a week and have work emails, they qualify.

My contact works part-time.

Contacts must work 30+ hours a week. Ask them to suggest a full-time coworker instead.

Can I use a family business?

Yes, as long as all three work there 30+ hours a week, have separate work emails, and fill the focal, coworker, and supervisor roles.

The supervisor is the business owner.

That is fine. The supervisor just needs to oversee the focal employee's work. Owners, managers, and directors all qualify.

I can't find anyone willing.

Most people say yes when asked, so try the email template below. If you truly can't find all three, the extended option needs only your focal employee. See the details.

Do they need to work at USC?

No. Contacts can be from any organization, as long as all three are at the same one.

What do my contacts actually do?

Each receives an email survey of about 30 minutes in late October, after Fall Recess. They can pause and resume, and nothing is in person.

What if one contact doesn't respond?

The research team sends automatic reminders, so you do not need to follow up. Let mor.research@usc.edu know if there is an issue.

Reaching Out

Copy a template, personalize it, and send. Most students hear back the same day.

My name is [Your Name] and I'm a student in USC Marshall's Organizational Behavior and Leadership course (BUAD 304).

As part of our coursework, I'm participating in a faculty research study on workplace dynamics, and I was hoping you could help. My deadline to submit names is October 2, 2026.

The research team needs survey responses from three people at the same organization: an employee I know (you), one of your coworkers, and your supervisor.

Best,
[Your Name]

Tips

  • Confirm all three will take part before you enter them in SONA.
  • Expect to ask 3 to 5 people before finding a match.
  • Tell your focal employee: "Watch for an email from mor.research@usc.edu in late October. It's legitimate USC research."

Knowing exactly what you are asking people to do makes the ask easier. Your contacts have their own site with details about the survey at bit.ly/mor-workplace. After you submit, each contact gets an email like this:

The survey takes approximately 30 minutes and can be paused and resumed at any time. Your responses are completely confidential and will never be shared with your employer, coworkers, or the student who nominated you.

Submit in SONA

Once all three names and emails are confirmed.

  1. 1

    Log in to SONA

    Go to marshall-mor.sona-systems.com and sign in.

  2. 2

    Find the Step 2B form

    After you finish prescreening (Step 2A), the contact form appears as a study listing in SONA. Click it to open.

  3. 3

    Enter all three contacts

    Fill in the name, work email, and job title for your focal employee, their coworker, and their supervisor.

  4. 4

    Submit, and you're done

    The research team reviews submissions, sends surveys in late October, and follows up automatically. You'll be notified when all three complete their surveys.

Double-check every email. Typos are the number one reason surveys never arrive. Verify each address with your contacts before submitting, since invalid emails cannot be corrected after the review period.

Can't Find Three Contacts?

You can switch to the Extended Lab Track, which only needs your focal employee.

Standard track

2.0 credits

  • Submit 3 employee contacts
  • Complete about 3–6 studies
  • Contacts due by Oct 2

Extended lab track

3.0 credits

  • Provide your focal employee's email only
  • Complete about 6–9 studies
  • No coworker or supervisor needed from you

A simpler extended option. You only provide your focal employee's email. The Lab team sends the outreach to their coworker and supervisor for you. You just need to have contacted your focal employee first and gotten their permission.

How to switch

  1. 1

    Get your focal employee's permission

    Reach out to someone you know at an organization, explain the study, and confirm they're willing to take part.

  2. 2

    Email the Lab team

    Send your focal employee's name and work email to mor.research@usc.edu and say you'd like the extended option.

  3. 3

    The Lab team handles the rest

    The research team contacts the coworker and supervisor for you, and you focus on completing 3.0 credits through studies.
